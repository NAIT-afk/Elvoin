About Elvoin Bakes

Our Story, Mission & Passion

Our Story

Founded in 2025 in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, Elvoin Bakes began with a simple dream: to create exceptional baked goods that bring joy to every celebration. Every creation that leaves our kitchen is made with premium ingredients, meticulous attention to detail, and a genuine passion for the art of baking.

Our Mission

To craft exceptional baked goods that create sweet memories using the finest ingredients and personalized service.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted bakery in the region, making every celebration sweeter with our handcrafted creations.

Our Philosophy

Every creation combines tradition with innovation, made with love and unwavering commitment to excellence.
